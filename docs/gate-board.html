Right now

The v1.4.1 gate did not move. It stands at 3 of 6 exactly as the 2026-09-04 board left it: criteria 1 and 2 passed on [A] 2026-08-25, criterion 3 on [A] 2026-09-04, criteria 4, 5 and 6 NOT RUN. The previous board's next action — ten minutes of owner dictation against the installed v1.4.1 plus one model download attempted under the firewall block — was not done. What happened instead, on 2026-09-04/05, was a rework of the floating dictation indicator ("the pill"), landed on main in twelve commits and recorded in RELEASING.md as Unreleased — queued for v1.5.0.

That rework found a defect in the published release. RELEASING.md (finding 1 of the new section, "2.7"): turning the status indicator off and on again in Settings freezes the app — every Tauri command hangs until the process is killed — because two synchronous commands could create a window, and tauri 2.10.3 documents that WebviewWindowBuilder::build() deadlocks inside a synchronous command on Windows. Reproduced twice on 2026-09-04. It is in the installed v1.4.0 and v1.4.1 builds; the fix (4fdb773, the three commands made async) is on main only. A second finding explains why the pill was sometimes invisible: tao 0.34.6 never raises a re-shown window, so any topmost window raised later covered it (reproduced with a stand-in; fixed by re-raising with SetWindowPos on every show). A third hypothesis — timer throttling in the hidden main webview — was measured and refuted (272 timers within 16 ms of schedule over 411 s). The owner ran the result with a microphone on 2026-09-05: English and Arabic dictation, badge, colours, hint and the new five-bar level meter all correct (docs/dictation-indicator-tests.md).

What that changes for the approver. First, the published v1.4.1 now carries four known defects until the next release: the vc_redist and WebView2 network paths, the sona-lock upgrade failure (all three recorded in RELEASING.md's v1.4.1 section), and the indicator re-enable deadlock — which is recorded only in the new Unreleased section, not in the v1.4.1 record and not in the public release notes. Second, the dictation the owner did on 2026-09-05 was on a dev build; by RELEASING.md's no-inheritance rule it counts for nothing on the v1.4.1 gate, whose criterion 5 still needs one English and one Arabic dictation into MS Word against the installed build. Third, the guarantee surface is unchanged and RELEASING.md says how it knows: git diff main on the four dependency files is empty, and the only new runtime traffic is two in-process Tauri events from Rust to the pill window.
